**My critique, briefly:** from across the room the sky is still a smooth, correct gradient, and the town reads more as cut paper than as distance. The notes cover the rest.

**Top five friction points:**
1. **Checkpoints go stale too easily.** Any edit to a helper function above `main` stales every checkpoint, so each tweak to the town's roofline or the knoll repainted the 95 s sky. The docs confirm that a stage's fingerprint covers "everything up to the closing brace of that block" (`paintings/src/run.rs`). My workaround was to wrap each helper in `// ckpt: from <stage>` … `// ckpt: end` and adopt old checkpoints with `--stale-ok --ckpt`. Also, a crop can't resume from the whole-canvas checkpoints; it needs its own set.
2. **There's no way to stop partway through a stage or skip one pass.** Soft olive blotches appeared all over the blue upper sky. To find the cause I had to add environment switches to my own program to turn the stipples off in a crop. The cause turned out to be thin blue over the warm ground, spread into blotches by the badger. A leaner, more covering dead color and lay-in cleared it.
3. **A "glaze" of pale paint turns milky.** `st.glaze(0.75)` with a pale warm color, meant as haze at the town's foot, turned the houses into white cottages in snow. Lead white scatters, so the veil was really an opaque layer. I moved the haze into the town's own color instead.
4. **Dry brush over dry paint lays slabs.** A nearly empty brush dragged lightly across the meadow laid flat, opaque patches that read as snow or scattered stones, even at +0.02 L. It didn't break up on the paint's grain. I removed the pass.
5. **Small light touches over a dark came out far too pale.** Touches aimed at `#554b45` over a tree crown dried to roughly `#7a7068` and turned the tree into a pale ghost. I removed them.

A small one to add: `Handling` has no `tool()` builder, so swapping a preset's brush needed a small extension trait in my program.
