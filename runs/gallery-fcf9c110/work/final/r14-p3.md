**Top five friction points** (details and workarounds in `notes/r14_p3.md`, FRICTION 1–5)
1. **The finish quietly grayed the sky.** The default aged varnish (`varnish_coats: 0.4`, `paintings/src/run.rs:676`) plus my umber veil took about 30 levels of blue out of the upper sky. The sky before the finish matched my plan within a few levels; I only found this by comparing pixel values. Workaround: 0.15 coats and a neutral dark veil (`r14_p3.rs:870`, `:884`).
2. **The engine's physical sky isn't a painter's dusk.** Just after sunset `atmos::Sky` gave a daytime blue with the glow spread evenly along the horizon, so I wrote my own color plan. Blending that plan as pigment also turned yellow-into-lilac muddy gray; blending it as light fixed it (`r14_p3.rs:219`).
3. **Code above `main` makes every checkpoint stale.** My scene builder and my diagnostics sat above the first stage, so moving a stone forced a full re-render of the sky. The fix was tagging them `// ckpt: from sea` / `from veil` (`r14_p3.rs:31`, `:278`, `:295`). Adopting stale checkpoints also takes one run per stage.
4. **Water gives thin things stub reflections and has one level.** `View::reflections` gave short pale stubs under the stakes, so I mirrored each stake myself (`r14_p3.rs:728`). A tide pool couldn't sit on the beach above sea level, so I removed it.
5. **The sky is about 80% of every render.** It took 85 s at first and 125 s by the end, against about 20 s for everything else, so it got the fewest looks. Each checkpoint is about 300–390 MB, 3.5 GB per `--ckpt` run.
