# Brief: which of these textures look "digital"?

These are 1:1 crops of painted skies from a simulated oil-paint engine
(simulated bristle brushes carrying paint over a canvas). Two sets:
- P01–P14: the same sky passage, 14 variants that differ only in one
  hidden rendering setting each.
- Q01–Q14: another sky passage, 14 variants, same idea.
The project's owner sees, in these skies, something she describes as "JPEG
artifacts" even though the files are lossless: "the entropy still reads
like JPEG artifacts", "it's something about texture". Look carefully at
every image (they are lossless PNGs; view them at full size).

For each set:
1. Rank the variants from the most "digital / JPEG-like / processed" to
   the most like real oil paint on canvas (all of them, one line each, with
   a few words on what you see).
2. Name the variants that clearly LOSE the JPEG-like look compared with
   most of the others, and describe exactly what visual property changed
   (e.g. blotches with sharp rims, speckle, color noise, flat patches with
   steps, a regular pattern, texture scale).
3. In one paragraph: what, visually, is the "JPEG artifact" look here?
Plain and concrete; no scores beyond the ranking. Write your answer to the
file named in your task, then reply with the same text.

IMPORTANT: look only at the image files in this folder. Do not open, list or search any other file or folder on this machine (no repository, no notes, no code): the judgment must be blind.
