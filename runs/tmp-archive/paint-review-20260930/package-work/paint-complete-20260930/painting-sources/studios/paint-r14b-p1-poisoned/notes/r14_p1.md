# r14_p1 — Evening in the Mountains (Abend im Gebirge)

## Composition and why
A wide evening view (1000 × 571 units, about 7:4) from a granite knoll over
receding ranges, the sun just gone down behind a saddle left of center.
- High sky, about three fifths of the height: cool smalt-gray above, a pale
  greenish-yellow middle, a warm glow low over the saddle (Friedrich's
  evening skies, stippled thin over a thin lay-in).
- Four ranges, each nearer one a step darker and cooler, mist lying at each
  foot (distance from value steps, as in the Riesengebirge pictures).
- A dark wooded shoulder on the right with firs as silhouettes against the
  valley mist.
- A contre-jour foreground knoll with a pile of granite boulders; on the top
  stone, a single figure seen from behind, dark against the glow opening
  (the Rückenfigur, small, off center).
- Grass flicked last over the knoll; a darker veil toward the picture's edges.
