  Practical lessons (hypotheses unless noted):
  - Pre-wet drops (5-8µL, r45-55, grid 80-95) plus rows of 50-70-cell segments topped up to 3.5-5µL at duration 0.02 give even, soft washes. Wash spill is about 30-50 cells past the polygon, so inset fills ≥25 cells where an edge must stay clean (observed).
  - cobalt-violet glazes over ochre/raw umber go salmon (observed). terre-verte .15/paynes .08/ochre .03 per 3.5µL neutralizes to khaki (observed).
  - buff-titanium is strongly opaque. At 1.2-1.6 per 1-1.5µL it makes crisp white lines over dark (good for road paint and poles). At .55 over black it gives loud lines (failed).
  - Wires: lamp-black .8/paynes .4, width 3, 0.4µL per 50-cell segment → crisp thin lines (observed).
  - Wall time: chunks of ~1500 strokes time out. Keep each chunk under ~800 strokes. Timed-out chunks don't commit.
  - The dark net (lamp-black .4/paynes .2/ultra .08 at 5µL, width 30) went nearly pure black. Next time use about half the strength and keep the row ends inside a soft pre-wet.
