# The painter's last reply

# *Lead and Brimstone at Deadwood Ridge*

Beneath a cold, moonlit desert canyon, a lone frontier gunslinger drops into a braced crouch, his heavy Colt revolver barking fire point-blank into the chest of a lunging undead monstrosity. The violent detonation of gunpowder tears open the darkness, projecting an incandescent, sculptural impasto core of cadmium yellow and lead white that illuminates the scene from within. Harsh golden rim light carves across the cowboy’s hat brim, clenched jaw, extended arm, and cylinder, while a pale, silvery lunar gleam washes his back and billowing duster coat. In the blast’s immediate path, the zombie reels backward in agonized kinetic shock—its desiccated green-gray skull, gaping teeth, and clawing hand searing in the flash as fractured ribs burst open and burning sparks and dark gore spray outward into the night air.

### Handoff & Studio Summary

#### 1. Status of Passages & Final Judgments
All passages marked for scrutiny at the close of Sitting 1 were reviewed under normal lighting and in detail crops during Sitting 2 and judged complete:
- **Ground Cast Shadows**: Directional cast shadows (`p_cast_shadow`) anchored under the cowboy’s boots (projecting west) and the zombie’s boots (projecting east) firmly plant both figures onto the terrain.
- **Desert Ground Texture**: Rake-lit stones and scattered gravel in the foreground exhibit crisp two-value definition—warm cadmium highlights facing the blast opposed by dark umber shadows.
- **Nocturnal Background & Topography**: The cool silvery moonlight cresting the distant left mesa establishes deep atmospheric recession and separates the cool night expanse from the warm foreground explosion.
- **Figure Accents & Refinements**: The sharp eye catchlight beneath the cowboy's Stetson, the brass cartridges at his belt, the spur glint, the drifting veil of gunsmoke, and the shredded fringes of decaying cloth on the zombie's reaching arm provide textural specificity without disrupting the primary tonal hierarchy.
