  - `work` with `piles` weights as functions of y gives a smooth, continuous vertical gradient in one pass — confirmed, no seams.
  - At coverage 3 the base passes leave the pale ground speckling through sky, water and bank (bright flecks). Fix: `fill=true` on the base passes.

  - Sky mid band (y 150–330) reads too pale/cream; deepened `sky mid` with cobalt and `sky deep` with black; `sky light` slightly less white.

  Uncertain: how the pencil horizon rule will show through the thin lay-in at the centre horizon (watch in crops); whether the moon will read at r≈12 in the whole view (judge in crop).
