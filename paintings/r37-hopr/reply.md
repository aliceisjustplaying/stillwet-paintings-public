# The painter's last reply

**Westbound, Sunday**

After fresh whole-canvas and detail inspections, I’m happy to leave it unchanged. The empty pavement and traveler’s lifted gaze hold the stillness; the spare glass and angular figure remain deliberate imperfections.

The journal records every acceptance and the handoff: leave the open road undisturbed, verify dryness before intervening, and test repairs on their intended substrate.
