> **Flooded Birch Wood, Evening**
> 
> The picture is a drowned birch wood at last light. Two dark birches are cut by the left edge, their bare limbs reaching into a grey evening sky that still carries the last of the cloud. A wide band of luminous vapour lies along the far shore with a dark wood standing behind it, and in front of that, the wood itself — a stand of bare birches standing in the flood in three ranks, dark in front and pale behind, their reflections running straight down into still water. The flood is open, with a swell or two, a drowned branch and a clump of reeds at the right; then a near bank with a fallen birch along it, tufts of grass, and a small figure standing at the water's edge, seen from behind.
> 
> The value range runs from about fifteen in the near trunks to about ninety in the vapour, with the dark band of the far wood between them — the thing my earlier notes said three sessions running that the picture lacked.
> 
> (the painter)
